# David's Journal: the words, world by world

Draft text for every checkpoint saying, every journal entry and every psalm line. **Glen edits here first; once a world is locked, he hand-writes its entries** (see JOURNAL.md for the layer rules).

**Rules for the words**
- Mark every made-up person with ✦ (e.g. Hanan ✦, the shepherd girl ✦). See DESIGN.md §3i.
- **Checkpoint sayings** are short, practical and in plain speech: one or two sentences about what's just ahead.
- **David's entries** are in his own words (a paraphrase, never a quotation), from scripture that existed **before** David: Genesis to Deuteronomy, Joshua, Judges, Ruth. The reference goes under each entry.
- Keep every entry short enough to hand-write: about 15 to 25 words.
- **Psalm lines** come from Glen's song "The Shepherd Leads Me".

## World 1: The Shepherd's Fields *(DRAFT, for Glen to edit)*

**The world's lesson:** the small, faithful jobs come first. Caring for one sheep, watching where you step, and not running from the lion are what God uses to prepare David.

### 1-1 · The Hills of Bethlehem
| | |
|---|---|
| **Learn** | Run, jump, sling. Aim up. **Look before you throw** (cobra vs green whip snake). |
| **Earn** | Aim-up skill (Shammah). |
| **Find** | 5 lost sheep, The One, 3 special stones. |
| **Checkpoint** | **Old Hanan** (first meeting) |

> **Hanan:** "The brown one with the hood will strike you. The thin green one won't hurt a soul, and it eats the rats. Look before you throw, boy."

**David writes:**
> *Not everything that moves in the grass is my enemy. But the serpent is crafty, so I will look before I throw.*
> — Genesis 3:1

**When David finds his first special stone (1-1):**
> **Hanan:** "Joshua stacked twelve stones by the Jordan so the children would ask what they meant. Keep that one. It'll remind you."

**David writes** (on the stone page of the Journal):
> *Twelve stones by the Jordan, so we would remember. I will keep my stones, and remember every time the LORD helped me.*
> — Joshua 4:6–7

### 1-2 · The Wandering Flock
| | |
|---|---|
| **Learn** | Gather lost sheep with the harp. Protect the line from foxes. Right a cast sheep. |
| **Earn** | The harp gathers the flock. |
| **Find** | 5 lost sheep, The One (in a low cave, comes back after 1-3), 3 special stones. |
| **Checkpoint** | **A neighbour shepherd** (an old man whose ram has wandered into your flock) |

> **Neighbour:** "That's my ram you've got with yours. Most boys would keep him. If you find your neighbour's sheep, you bring it home."

**David writes:**
> *If my neighbour's sheep wanders, I must not look away. I bring it home, even when it isn't mine.*
> — Deuteronomy 22:1–3

### 1-3 · The Rocky Pastures
| | |
|---|---|
| **Learn** | The roll (under the ram's charge and through low caves). Crumbling ledges, rope bridges, hidden paths. |
| **Earn** | The roll (Shammah). |
| **Find** | 5 lost sheep, The One, 3 special stones, a hidden cave. |
| **Checkpoint** | **The shepherd girl** (her goat is stuck up a rock) |

> **Shepherd girl:** "My grandmother says these rocks were here before anyone. Storms come and go, and they don't move. She says God is like that."

**David writes:**
> *The LORD is my Rock. Everything He does is right, and He does not move.*
> — Deuteronomy 32:4

### 1-4 · The Lion's Territory (night) + the Lion
| | |
|---|---|
| **Learn** | Jackals at night, darkness, the lion's warning (crouch, tail-flick, roar), dodge the pounce, hit it while dazed. |
| **Earn** | The lamb is rescued (it becomes player 2's lamb). **World gift: Jesse's old sling.** +1 heart. |
| **Find** | 5 lost sheep, The One, 3 special stones. |
| **Checkpoint** | **Hanan**, at the last fire |

> **Hanan:** "You're of Judah, and Judah's sign is the lion. Don't run from this one. Watch for the crouch, and the tail."

**David writes:**
> *I am of Judah, the lion's cub. The LORD goes ahead of me, so I will not be afraid.*
> — Genesis 49:9; Deuteronomy 31:8

### End of World 1: the Journal spread
- **Left page (Wisdom):** the four entries above, written in one after another.
- **Right page (the Psalm):** the first line of the song:
> *The Lord is my shepherd,*
> *I have everything I need.*
- **The stone count:** *"Stones of remembrance: 9 of 12"* (how many of World 1's 12 you found). All 12 raises a stone cairn on the World 1 map.
- **Music:** the harp opening of the song (the first 15 seconds).

**Optional bonus entry** (after beating the lion), if there's room on the left page:
> *Samson met a lion too, and the LORD gave him strength. Tonight He gave me mine.*
> — Judges 14:5–6

## Note: the old stage scrolls
The first draft of World 1 had a hidden scroll per stage with verses like Psalm 23:1, Luke 16:10 and Psalm 121. Psalm 23 is the song David is *writing*, and the others come centuries later, so they don't fit as things David finds. **Suggestion:** drop the stage scrolls and let the checkpoint sayings and David's entries be the scripture in each stage. The later verses can live on as Field Guide or Bible-fact cards.
