# The Shepherd Leads Me

Glen's song, a paraphrase of Psalm 23 in his own words (made on Suno). Presented in the game as *"The Shepherd Leads Me, a song inspired by Psalm 23."* David writes it in his Journal one world at a time (see JOURNAL_TEXT.md); the instrumental plays in Worlds 1–7 and the full vocal only at the end.

**Rights note:** check the Suno plan the song was made on before any commercial release.

**[Verse 1]**
The Lord is my shepherd,
I have everything I need.
He brings me to the quiet places,
Where my weary heart can breathe.
He leads me beside the waters,
Where the troubled waves grow still.
He restores my soul again,
And guides me where He will.

**[Chorus]**
You are with me, You are with me,
I will never walk alone.
Through the darkest valley,
You will lead me safely home.
I will not be afraid,
For Your presence is my strength.
Your hand will guide me forward,
Every step and every day.

**[Verse 2]**
You lead me down the right road,
For the honor of Your name.
Even when the shadows gather,
You remain the same.
When the darkness surrounds me,
And I cannot see the way,
I know Your hand is on me,
And Your rod will keep me safe.

**[Verse 3]**
You set a table before me,
Even while my enemies stand near.
You pour Your blessing over me,
And You replace my fear.
You fill my cup with goodness,
More than I could ever hold.
Your mercy keeps on following me,
And Your love will never grow old.

**[Chorus]** (repeats)

**[Outro]**
Surely Your goodness will find me,
All the days that I am here.
And when this life is over,
I know You will still be near.
I will live within Your presence,
I will rest beneath Your care.
My Shepherd, Lord and Savior,
I will dwell with You forever.
